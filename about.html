About me

My name is Eyal, I’m a designer, drummer, tattoo artist and I used to know how to solve a Rubik’s cube. Based in Tel Aviv, and currently studying at Shenkar College of Engineering, Design and Art.

I’m drawn to a wide range of aesthetics, from the extremely clean and structured to the messy, crude, and off-grid.

When I’m not designing, I’m usually playing with my band “Cult of the Meerkat”, or walking my dog, Boni.

Reach out for collaborations, design work, or to talk about Avatar the Last Airbender.

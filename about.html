About me

My name is Eyal, I’m a designer, drummer, tattoo artist and I once knew how to solve a Rubik’s cube.

I’m drawn to a wide range of aesthetics, from the extremely clean and structured to the messy, crude, and off-grid.

When I’m not designing, I’m usually playing with my band or walking my dog, Boni.

Based in Tel Aviv, and currently studying at Shenkar College of Engineering, Design and Art.

Reach out for collaborations, design work, or to talk about Avatar the Last Airbender.

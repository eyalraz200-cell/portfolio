Nergal - 2300 BCE

Manifested as destructive heat, he is linked to plague and war, where fire appears not as flame but as an overwhelming force that spreads death and devastation.

Dagon - 2500 BCE

Represented with fish-like features, he is one of the few figures in the pantheon that appears in both Mesopotamian and biblical traditions.

Ninurta - 2600 BCE

Manifested as storm and weapon, he channels wind and thunder into tools of war, using them in mythic battles to defeat monstrous forces and restore order to the world.

Kingu - 2000 BCE

Tiamat’s general and consort, manifested as earth and stone, he carried the Tablet of Destinies on his chest - the document that gave its holder power over all creation - and lost it.

Tiamat - 2000 BCE

Manifested as the primordial saltwater ocean, she is the only god in the pantheon who had to die for the world to exist - her body split in two, one half becoming the sky, the other the earth.

Shamash - 3000 BCE

Apearing as the sun crossing the sky from mountain to mountain, he saw every transaction and every lie, which is why Hammurabi claimed it was Shamash, not himself, who wrote the law.
